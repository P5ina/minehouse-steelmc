Conversion of vanilla player saves (players/data, players/stats, players/advancements).

Vanilla 26.x keeps one <uuid>.dat per player plus <uuid>.json stats and advancements. Each player is written to Steel’s player storage of one domain, and the domain becomes the player’s last active domain.
