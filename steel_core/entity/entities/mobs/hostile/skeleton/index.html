Module skeleton 

Source
Expand description

Skeleton, stray, bogged, wither skeleton and parched entity module. Vanilla AbstractSkeleton and its kinds: Skeleton, Stray, Bogged, WitherSkeleton and Parched.

All five share the bow-or-melee weapon goals, the sun-avoiding goals and the arrow shot; each kind changes its sounds, its arrows and a few rules.
