Expand description

Horses, donkeys, mules and the undead horses. Vanilla AbstractHorse and the equines built on it.

Horses, donkeys, mules, skeleton horses and zombie horses share taming, riding, feeding and breeding through the AbstractHorse trait; each concrete entity only supplies its sounds, offspring and attributes.
