Vanilla AbstractArrow: the stuck-in-ground arrow physics, piercing, critical hits, pickup and despawn shared by arrows and spectral arrows.

Concrete arrows embed an ArrowBase and implement AbstractArrow; their Entity::tick calls AbstractArrow::abstract_arrow_tick (vanilla super.tick()).
